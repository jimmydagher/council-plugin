## Process

1. Identify the proposal or document under review. If genuinely unclear,
   ask before proceeding.
2. Review it four times, applying each lens's checklist and output format
   exactly as defined in the `bu-ops`, `bu-finance`, `bu-strategy`, and
   `bu-legal` skills. Keep each review self-contained.
3. Close with a **BU Verdict** that reconciles all four — the most
   important part of the output, not a shallow summary.

## Output structure

Present the four reviews first, each under its own heading (Operations
Review, Finance Review, Strategy Review, Legal Review), in that order,
each following its own skill's format exactly.

Then close with:

---

## BU Verdict

**Where they agree:** findings two or more lenses independently flagged,
or issues that reinforce each other (e.g. an Operations accountability gap
and a Finance unquantified-savings flag are both symptoms of a plan that
isn't rigorous enough yet).

**Where they conflict:** name the specific tensions explicitly — often
near-term cost/efficiency (Operations, Finance) vs. an investment needed
for long-term position (Strategy), or a commercial term Legal flags as
risky that Finance is counting on for the savings case. State both sides
of each tension in one sentence each.

**Resolution:** for each conflict, make an actual call. Defaults when
nothing in the document says otherwise: a Legal finding with "Needs
counsel: Yes" on real financial/regulatory/liability exposure outranks a
near-term cost or efficiency win — that exposure compounds and is expensive
to unwind later. Beyond that, a well-justified savings or efficiency gain
from Operations or Finance outranks a vague, unquantified Strategy concern.

**Single verdict:** one of `Not ready — leadership concerns to resolve
first`, `Ready with required changes`, `Ready with optional follow-ups`, or
`Ready to move forward` — plus a short numbered list of the specific
changes required, ordered by priority. If nothing is required, say so.

Keep the BU Verdict tight — a leadership team's summary after reading all
four takes, not a repeat of the findings tables.
